2. Information Stored by IQworkout

IQworkout does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, telephone number, address, or other personal information in order to use the App.

The App stores certain game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • game points;
  • unlocked levels;
  • completed levels;
  • level attempts;
  • information related to rewarded-ad cooldowns.

This information is used solely to provide and maintain the functionality of the game.

3. Advertising

IQworkout uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, information about interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic information, in accordance with Google's policies.

The processing of such information is performed by Google and may be subject to Google's own privacy policy and consent mechanisms.

More information about Google's data practices can be found in Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law, IQworkout may request your consent before personalized advertising is displayed.

Advertising preferences may be managed through the consent options presented by the App.

5. Data Sharing

IQworkout does not sell your personal information.

The App does not operate its own advertising or analytics servers. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob as described above.

6. Data Security

Game progress stored by IQworkout is stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server that stores your game progress.

7. Children's Privacy

IQworkout is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights regarding personal data processed by third-party service providers, including rights of access, deletion, restriction of processing or objection.
